Our story

People are more than the labels they carry.

Wambles of Wales grew from a simple belief: everybody deserves the chance to contribute, belong and be treated with dignity. Based in Cardiff, we make space for people experiencing homelessness and anyone who wants to help.

Wamble with us.

Our name is a reminder to keep moving, keep laughing and keep making room. We are a community group with practical projects, open invitations and a stubborn optimism about what people can do together.
